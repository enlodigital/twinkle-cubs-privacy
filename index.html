Six Little Cubs Playground Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

About Six Little Cubs Playground

Six Little Cubs Playground is a children’s game for Android developed and operated by Mark Iannello, an Australian sole trader. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, contact support or visit this privacy page.

Information We Collect

The game has no account registration or login, and it does not require an email address to play. The developer does not automatically receive the nickname, artwork or gameplay progress saved in the game.

Gameplay information is saved locally on the device, as described below. Google Play Billing communicates with Google to check Full Game ownership, handle purchases and restoration, and send technical Billing events and diagnostics. These processes are separate from local gameplay saves. See Google Play Billing below.

If a parent emails support, the parent’s email address and any information they choose to include are sent through their email service to the developer’s support mailbox. The developer uses support messages to respond to help, feedback and privacy requests and to keep relevant support records. Please avoid including a child’s real name or other unnecessary personal information in a support message.

Visiting this privacy webpage is separate from playing the game. Website hosting is described under Third-Party Services.

Information Stored on the Device

The game stores information in its own app storage to personalise play and let a player continue later. This includes:

  • An optional name or nickname entered for on-screen greetings. A real name is not required, and the game can be played without entering a name.
  • The selected Cub and game preferences, such as sound and music settings.
  • Local age/content-group choices used to select activity options.
  • Game progress, scores, completed pictures, unlocked activities, tutorial status and tracing or connect-the-dots progress.
  • The Twinkles balance, Twinkle Cards collection, earned packs and local reward history.
  • Twinkle Sticker Album collections, sticker placements and saved sticker scenes.
  • Saved drawings, colouring, artwork drafts and related gallery information.
  • A local Full Game ownership record, including the product identifier, purchase token supplied by Google Play and acknowledgement status, used to remember purchase access and complete purchase confirmation.
  • Technical caches and queued diagnostic reports used by the Google Play Billing library.

The game does not upload or synchronise your gameplay saves, artwork or collections to a developer account or cloud-save service. Full Game purchase restoration through Google Play is separate: it restores access, not those existing local saves. The app disables Android backup and excludes its data from configured cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. Device manufacturers’ separate transfer tools may behave differently.

Some activities offer content-group choices to select suitable activity options on the device. This feature does not ask for a child's date of birth and does not verify age or identity. The selection is used locally and is not automatically sent to the developer or another service.

Children’s Privacy

The game is designed for young children. It does not include child accounts, social messaging, public profiles or a feature for uploading children’s creations to the developer or other players.

A nickname is optional. A real name is not required, and a player can leave the name blank. The game does not request access to the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

Settings, purchase and restoration controls, support email actions and the privacy-policy link are in a Parent Area protected by a reading check. This check is a navigation barrier for grown-ups; it is not identity verification or a parental-consent service. Parents should supervise use of the game and any external email or browser app.

If a parent includes information about a child in a support email, the developer receives that information as part of the correspondence. Please share only what is necessary to explain the issue.

Advertising

There are no advertisements or advertising SDKs in this version of the game.

Analytics and Diagnostics

The game does not use advertising, gameplay analytics or third-party crash-reporting services. The developer does not receive your nickname, saved artwork or gameplay progress automatically. The Google Play Billing library sends technical events and diagnostics to Google, as described in the Google Play Billing section.

Gameplay loading, timing and playback messages created by the game are not automatically sent to the developer. This is separate from Google Play Billing’s own technical reporting and the report a parent may choose to copy and share. Android, Google Play and device providers also operate services under their own policies.

After using Restore Purchase, a parent can choose Copy purchase check to place a technical report on the device clipboard. It includes app version, app and installer identifiers, purchase-query results and purchase/acknowledgement status. Purchase tokens, raw purchase records, signatures and order IDs are omitted or redacted. The report is held in memory and is not automatically uploaded or sent to support. If you choose to share it through email or another service, that service handles the information under its own policies, and any support copy the developer receives is handled as support correspondence.

Third-Party Services

The game uses Android device features for local rendering, storage and audio playback. It may read device tilt while certain shiny-card effects are visible. Tilt readings are used temporarily for the visual effect and are not saved or sent to the developer. The game does not use active Text-to-Speech, speech recognition or microphone recording in this version.

The device keyboard used to enter a nickname is supplied by Android or the keyboard provider. Its operation is governed by that provider's settings and policies; the game does not control those separate services.

Google Play provides purchase, restoration and Billing technical-reporting services, as described in the Google Play Billing section. Google handles information under its own terms and policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

The developer’s support address uses Gmail. If you send an email, your email provider and Google's Gmail service handle that correspondence under their own policies. The game itself opens an email draft addressed to support; it does not automatically send a message or attach the player's name, saves, artwork or logs.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub provides the website hosting and may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and operate the website under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The page contains no advertising, analytics code, tracking scripts, forms or externally loaded fonts. Website request processing is separate from local gameplay saves.

Google Play Billing

Purchases and restoration

Full Game is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play, accessed through the parent-controlled area. The game has no subscriptions or paid Twinkles. Twinkles and in-game reward items use the game's earned-reward system.

Google Play handles checkout, payment information and the Google account used for a purchase. The game does not receive your payment-card details or Google account email through its Billing integration. It receives purchase information from Google Play, including the product identifier, purchase token, purchase state and acknowledgement status, to check and restore Full Game access and confirm purchases. A purchase-ownership record is retained in private app storage. The developer can view order information made available in Google Play Console for purchase administration and support.

The game communicates with Google Play to obtain product information and localised prices, check ownership, restore access and acknowledge purchases. Ownership checks may also occur when opening or returning to the game. It does not include the entered child/player name, artwork, gameplay progress or collections in its Billing requests. It does not send purchase information to a developer-operated server.

Restore Purchase checks ownership with Google Play. Purchase access depends on the Google account used for the purchase and the account context supplied by Play. Restoring Full Game does not transfer existing gameplay progress, artwork, Twinkles, cards or stickers between devices or Android user profiles.

Billing technical events and diagnostics

The Google Play Billing library uses Google DataTransport to send technical Billing events and diagnostics to Google. These can include app/package and Billing-library versions, request outcomes and timing, a Billing-session identifier, and device/Android-build, network and locale information.

Device and technical information can include Android version, device model and manufacturer, hardware and operating-system build information, timezone, network type, language, country and SIM operator code. The operating-system build fingerprint is not a unique hardware identifier, and the SIM operator code is not a telephone number.

The library can queue these reports in private app storage before transmission. Reporting can occur during purchase-ownership checks when opening or returning to the game, even if no new purchase is started.

This reporting is separate from the optional Copy purchase check report and local gameplay saves. Google handles these records under its terms and privacy policy.

Saved Artwork

Drawings, colouring and saved sticker scenes are stored in the game's private app storage on the device. The game does not automatically publish them, upload them or save them to a public photo gallery. It does not request access to the device's existing photos or media library.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local saves remain available between play sessions until removed through the game’s relevant controls, Android’s app-storage controls or uninstalling the app.

Delete All My Data

For complete removal of the game’s local data, a parent can open Settings, complete the Parent Area check and choose Delete All My Data, then confirm.

This asks Android to clear the application’s stored data, including the nickname, selected Cub, settings, artwork, game saves, local purchase-ownership record, SDK caches and queued diagnostic reports.

Android closes the game when clearing succeeds. Reopening the game starts setup again. If the request cannot be completed, the game reports that it could not clear the data. Parents can also use Android Settings to clear the app’s storage.

Reset saves, scores & collections

Reset saves, scores & collections is different from complete deletion. It clears most gameplay information in the main game save and removes saved colouring, drawing and sticker-scene files.

It keeps the nickname, selected Cub, sound and haptic preferences, and once-only Diamond bonus state. It does not clear the separate Spot the Difference save, the Full Game ownership record or SDK caches.

Use Delete All My Data for complete local removal.

Google records and purchase access

Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling removes local game data and the local purchase-ownership record. Resetting gameplay saves or clearing local data does not cancel or refund a Full Game purchase. Full Game access can be checked again through Google Play using the purchasing account.

Clearing app storage also removes local SDK caches and queued diagnostic reports. It does not delete records already received by Google, including Billing technical diagnostics and transaction records. The developer’s support-mail deletion process does not delete those Google records either. Google controls retention and deletion of its records under its privacy policy.

Support correspondence and website records

Deleting local app data does not delete emails you have sent to support or records held by your email provider or website host.

I keep support emails for as long as needed to respond to your request, troubleshoot reported issues and keep relevant support records. You can request deletion by emailing enlodigital.apps@gmail.com.

Your email provider and the website host handle their own records under their own policies.

Security

The game uses Android's private app-storage facilities. It does not provide player accounts or a gameplay cloud-save service. These protections do not make any device or email service completely secure. Parents should keep their device protected and consider what they include in support messages.

External Links

The Parent Area can open an email app for support. The Privacy Policy option in the Parent Area opens this webpage in the device browser. The game does not append the player's name, progress or other saved information to that link.

The email app, browser, website host and device keyboard are separate services with their own settings and policies. The game does not provide these external actions directly in child-facing gameplay.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

The developer may update this policy if the game or its privacy practices change. The updated policy will show its publication or update date. Changes involving online services, analytics, advertising, accounts or purchases require a fresh privacy and Google Play Data Safety review.
