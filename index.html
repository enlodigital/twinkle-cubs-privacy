Twinkle Cubs Playground Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

About Twinkle Cubs Playground

Twinkle Cubs Playground is a children's mobile game developed by Enlo Digital, intended primarily for children ages 4–8. This policy explains the information used by the current Android game, its support contact and this privacy webpage.

Information We Collect

The game does not automatically send player names, artwork, game progress or device information to us. It has no account registration or login, and it does not require an email address to play. Gameplay information is saved locally on the device, as described below.

If a parent emails support, the parent's email address and any information they choose to include are sent through their email service to our support mailbox. We use support messages to respond to help, feedback and privacy requests and to keep records of reported faults and troubleshooting. Please avoid including a child's real name or other unnecessary personal information in a support message.

Visiting this privacy webpage is separate from playing the game. Website hosting is described under Third-Party Services.

Information Stored on the Device

The game stores information in its own app storage to personalise play and let a player continue later. This includes:

  • The name or nickname entered for on-screen greetings. A real name is not needed; a nickname or the default name “Cub” can be used.
  • The selected Cub and game preferences, such as sound and music settings.
  • Game progress, scores, completed pictures, unlocked activities, tutorial status and tracing or connect-the-dots progress.
  • The Twinkles balance, Twinkle Cards collection, earned packs and local reward history.
  • Twinkle Sticker Album collections, sticker placements and saved sticker scenes.
  • Saved drawings, colouring, artwork drafts and related gallery information.

The current game does not upload or synchronise these saves to a developer account or cloud service. There is no cloud-save or cross-device account feature. The app disables Android backup and excludes its data from configured cloud-backup and device-transfer rules.

Children's Privacy

The game is designed for young children. It does not include child accounts, social messaging, public profiles or a feature for uploading children's creations to us or other players. It does not request access to the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

Settings, support email actions and the privacy-policy link are in a Parent Area protected by a reading check. This check is a navigation barrier for grown-ups; it is not identity verification or a parental-consent service. Parents should supervise use of the game and any external email or browser app.

Advertising

There are no advertisements or advertising SDKs in this version of the game.

Analytics and Tracking

The game does not use analytics SDKs, behavioural tracking, advertising identifiers or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not send gameplay events, progress or artwork to an analytics service.

Some local diagnostic messages describe loading, timing or playback errors. The game has no feature that uploads these logs to us. Android, Google Play and device providers may operate their own services under their own policies.

Third-Party Services

The game uses Android device features for local rendering, storage and audio playback. It may read device tilt while certain shiny-card effects are visible. Tilt readings are used temporarily for the visual effect and are not saved or sent to us. The game does not use active Text-to-Speech, speech recognition or microphone recording in this version.

The device keyboard used to enter a nickname is supplied by Android or the keyboard provider. Its operation is governed by that provider's settings and policies; the game does not control those separate services.

Our developer and support contact addresses use Gmail. If you send an email, your email provider and Google's Gmail service handle that correspondence under their own policies. The game itself opens an email draft addressed to support; it does not automatically send a message or attach the player's name, saves, artwork or logs.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub provides the website hosting and may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and operate the website under its own privacy policy. The page contains no advertising, analytics code, tracking scripts, forms or externally loaded fonts. Website request processing is separate from local gameplay saves.

Purchases

This version does not include real-money in-app purchases or Google Play Billing. Twinkles and in-game shop items use the game's local earned-reward system. No payment-card or billing details are requested by the game.

If real-money purchases or Google Play Billing are added later, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will need to be reviewed before that version is released.

Saved Artwork

Drawings, colouring and saved sticker scenes are stored in the game's private app storage on the device. The game does not automatically publish them, upload them or save them to a public photo gallery. It does not request access to the device's existing photos or media library.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local saves remain available between play sessions until removed through the game's relevant controls, Android's app-storage controls or uninstalling the app.

For complete removal of the game's local data, a parent can open Settings, complete the Parent Area check and choose Delete All My Data, then confirm. This asks Android to clear the application's stored data, including the name, selected Cub, settings, artwork and game saves. Android closes the game when clearing succeeds. Reopening the game starts setup again. Parents can also use Android Settings to clear the app's storage.

Reset saves, scores & collections is different from complete deletion. It retains the name, selected Cub and sound preferences, and it is not a substitute for Delete All My Data. Some activity progress may remain after a Reset. Use complete deletion when you want all local game data removed.

Deleting local app data does not delete emails you have sent to support or records held by your email provider or website host. We retain support emails as records of reported faults and troubleshooting. If you request deletion using the support address in Contact below, we will delete your support correspondence from our support mailbox. Your email provider and the website host handle their own records under their own policies.

Security

The game uses Android's private app-storage facilities. It does not provide player accounts or a cloud-save service. These protections do not make any device or email service completely secure. Parents should keep their device protected and consider what they include in support messages.

External Links

The Parent Area can open an email app for support. The Privacy Policy option in the Parent Area opens this webpage in the device browser. The game does not append the player's name, progress or other saved information to that link.

The email app, browser, website host and device keyboard are separate services with their own settings and policies. The game does not provide these external actions directly in child-facing gameplay.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if the game or its privacy practices change. The updated policy will show its publication or update date. Changes involving online services, analytics, advertising, accounts or purchases require a fresh privacy and Google Play Data Safety review.
